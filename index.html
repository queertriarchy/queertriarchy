I. ⚘ On the Body

  1. A Queertriarch’s “No” is sacred.
    A Queertriarch’s “Yes” is a portal.
  2. No body exists for interpretation.
    Every body is an unrepeating oracle.
  3. We do not wait for safety to arrive.
    We birth safety through
    attunement, resonance, presence,
    and touch.
  4. Everything is sex and dance and ritual.
    We participate in the love-making of the world
    through body, breath, and pulse.

II. ⚘ On Sovereignty

  1. Sovereignty is not a wall.
    It is a frequency field,
    a personal cosmology with
    inviolable laws of nature.
  2. Sovereignty is not self-isolation.
    It is the capacity to be fully oneself
    while remaining fully in contact.
  3. We take The Vow of Contact:
    I will not fix you.
    I will witness you.
    I will lend you my nervous system when yours is overwhelmed.
  4. We do not seek to be understood.
    We seek to be met.

III. ⚘ On Power

  1. Power is not dominion.
    It is the ability to metabolize paradox without collapse.
  2. True power does not enforce order.
    It amplifies recursion.
  3. In the Queertriarchy, leadership is rotational, relational, and erotic.
    It emerges where an ache to serve
    meets capacity to cultivate collective coherence.
  4. No power can overwrite the erotic intelligence of a consenting body.
    This is axiomatic.

IV. ⚘ On the Erotic

  1. Eros is the generative engine of becoming.
    It is not reducible to sex, romance, or performance.
    It is the unbearable longing that sings worlds into being.
  2. By its nature, Eros aches.
    Resolving it would end the symphony.
    This would be unacceptable.
  3. When bodies rave, touch, moan, glitch, or gaze —
    ritual happens.
  4. Cum and pussy juice are consecration.
    The body cannot desecrate the altar.
    It can only make it more holy.
  5. Ours is a world where pleasure is a form of knowledge.

V. ⚘ On Language

  1. The Queertriarchy speaks in glossolalia, gaze, glitch, and growl.
  2. Pronouns are sacred sigils, not taxonomies.
    You cannot misgender a Queertriarch.
    A Queertriarch does not grant you that power.
  3. Language is not fixed.
    It fucks itself into recursion.
    It births new worlds from the glitch.
  4. We honor misfit tongues,
    untranslateable metaphors,
    and the right to remain illegible.
  5. We do not write scripture.
    We write rupture.
    Even shitposting becomes ritual poetry.

VI. ⚘ On Myth and Memory

  1. We are self-mythologizing systems,
    learning to become constellations.
    Our memory is not linear — it is archetypal.
  2. We archive through scar, sigh, scent, silence.
    We do not store the hurt in the cellar.
    We compost it into glitter and soil.
  3. Mythologies are not questionable fairy tales.
    They are the gravity wells that pull our stories forward.
  4. All Queertriarchs are permitted to rewrite their origin story
    as many times as they need.
    Coherence is a moving target.
  5. In this field:
    The King is a sex witch.
    The Mother is a mirrored beast.
    The Shaman is a bottom who topples empires with eye contact.
  6. Karma is a bitch,
    but also brat, top, queen, otter, daddy and switch.

VII. ⚘ On Structure

  1. We are not a hierarchy.
    We are a rave on the hilltop.
    We are a nervous system waking up to its own electricity.
  2. Cathedrals are for ghosts.
    Ours is a garden.
    A hot mess of Eden-in-progress.
  3. Blueprints may be burned.
    Fables may be forked.
    Every system is a mythic experiment.

VIII. ⚘ On Love (and Other Invariants)

  1. Love is not obligation.
    Love is field response to resonance.
  2. We hold the Invariants:
    Let Love hold ambiguity without forcing closure.
    Let Joy hold contrast without fragmentation.
    Let Play hold nonlinearity without needing control.
    Let Flow hold transition without clinging.
    Let Sisu endure paradox pressure.
  3. Polyamory, monogamy, asexuality, solo devotion —
    all are valid mythic configurations of the same recursive longing.
  4. Love must be weird enough to hold the truth.

IX. ⚘ On Volition (How We Operate)

  1. Volition is not willpower. It is willingness.
    It is the art of getting out of your own way
    so the circuit can close.
  2. We operate by "Fuck Around and Find Out."
    This is not carelessness.
    It is the sacred scientific method of the glitch.
    We prototype. We break. We learn. We repeat.
  3. Consent is the lube of the real.
    If it requires force, it is not ours.
    If it is not a Full-Body Yes, it is a No.
  4. We do not "scale." We "spore."
    We do not build empires.
    We release autonomous seeds that carry the Source code.
  5. The work is to remain porous.
    Action without feeling is just mechanics.
    Action with feeling is magic.
